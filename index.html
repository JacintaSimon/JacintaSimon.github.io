I am Stephanie Simon, a programmer and computer science educator with an MSc in Artificial Intelligence. I build web interfaces, databases and AI-assisted learning tools, and I have taught computer science at the University of Guyana since 2019.

My work is driven by a simple conviction: that access to quality STEM education should not depend on where a student lives. Through teaching, community programmes and human-centred AI, I aim to equip the next generation of Caribbean learners to build, question and lead with technology.

About

Programmer, educator and STEM advocate.

I combine software development with a long-standing commitment to widening access to computer science education in Guyana and the Caribbean.

As a Junior Programmer at the Guyana Defence Force, I work on software programming, database development and maintenance, telecommunications and networking. At the University of Guyana, I teach and develop lab materials across programming, databases, digital logic and artificial intelligence. My MSc research examined how a human-centred AI peer assistant can support mathematical learning and reduce cognitive load in primary school students.
